scrypt-jane
===========

hashcat compiles in scrypt-jane for the scrypt based hash modes. The source is in
deps/scrypt-jane-master and states its terms in the first lines of scrypt-jane.c:

  /*
      scrypt-jane by Andrew M, https://github.com/floodyberry/scrypt-jane

      Public Domain or MIT License, whichever is easier
  */

Its README repeats the same statement under the heading License.

Where the MIT half is taken, its text is the one in docs/license.txt.
